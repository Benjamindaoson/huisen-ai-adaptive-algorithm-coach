## ADDED Requirements\n\n### Requirement: Post-transfer action SHALL open the exact next teachable lesson\n\nThe system SHALL reuse the authoritative prerequisite progress projection to bind the post-transfer action to one started, checkpoint-passed, or available foundation lesson.\n\n#### Scenario: First foundation lesson is available\n\n- **WHEN** immediate transfer is complete and no foundation lesson has been completed\n- **THEN** the post-transfer action opens `input-output` directly and names that lesson\n\n#### Scenario: A prerequisite was completed\n\n- **WHEN** `input-output` is complete and `variables-state` is available\n- **THEN** the post-transfer action opens `variables-state` directly and does not open the full curriculum map first\n\n#### Scenario: No foundation lesson is available\n\n- **WHEN** the authoritative progress projection yields no next lesson\n- **THEN** the system falls back to the learning center without inventing a locked destination\n\n
