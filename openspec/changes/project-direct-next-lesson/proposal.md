# Change: Project the exact next lesson after transfer\n\n## Why\n\nThe post-transfer action currently opens the full learning center. Although that page marks one current task, it also presents dozens of modules and paths, forcing a learner who just completed a session to search and click again.\n\n## What Changes\n\n- Reuse the existing prerequisite progress projection to identify the one next teachable foundation lesson.\n- Bind the post-transfer action's label and route to that lesson.\n- Keep the learning-center fallback when no lesson is available.\n- Preserve the full learning center for voluntary exploration.\n\n## Impact\n\n- Affected specs: `projected-learning-action`\n- Affected code: projected action contract/tests and consuming Insights/Mentor surfaces\n\n
