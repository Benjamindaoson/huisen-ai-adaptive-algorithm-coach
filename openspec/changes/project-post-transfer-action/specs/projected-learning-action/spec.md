## ADDED Requirements\n\n### Requirement: Completed immediate transfer SHALL override onboarding and in-progress fallbacks\n\nThe system SHALL project one post-training action from the newest valid immediate-transfer completion and SHALL bind that action to its exact evidence reference without promoting the event to mastery.\n\n#### Scenario: Transfer exists without entrance diagnosis\n\n- **WHEN** a valid `training-stage-completed: transfer` event exists and no mastery evidence exists\n- **THEN** Insights and Mentor acknowledge the completed immediate transfer and recommend `选择下一项训练` rather than restarting diagnosis\n\n#### Scenario: Transfer follows diagnosis and training start\n\n- **WHEN** older diagnosis and training-start events exist before the immediate-transfer completion\n- **THEN** the completion action wins and its destination opens the learning center\n\n
