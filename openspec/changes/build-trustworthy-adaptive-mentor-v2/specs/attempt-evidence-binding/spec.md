## ADDED Requirements\n\n### Requirement: Mentor analysis is bound to one attempt\nThe problem workspace SHALL identify the exact attempt, timestamp, language, mode, and outcome used by the Mentor and SHALL continue analyzing its immutable source snapshot.\n\n#### Scenario: Editor changed after submission\n- **WHEN** the current editor source differs from the analyzed attempt snapshot\n- **THEN** the workspace SHALL label the analysis as stale and provide a readable bounded diff without mutating the attempt\n\n### Requirement: Evidence authority is visible\nThe Mentor timeline SHALL distinguish verified observations, unverified hypotheses, missing evidence, and tests that were actually executed.\n\n#### Scenario: Diagnosis lacks verification\n- **WHEN** a Mentor conclusion has no confirming execution evidence\n- **THEN** the UI SHALL present it as a hypothesis and SHALL NOT use language implying that the cause is proven\n\n
