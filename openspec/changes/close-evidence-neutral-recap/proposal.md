## Why\n\nQuick recap currently lets a learner replay the explanation and return, but it never asks what understanding changed. The experience therefore ends like passive content consumption: the learner cannot visibly close the misconception loop, and the product cannot distinguish “opened recap” from “formed a corrected mental model.”\n\n## What Changes\n\n- Add a bounded “现在我明白了” reflection after the learner revisits the human-language explanation.\n- Ask the learner to distinguish the corrected mental model from the original misconception.\n- Bind the reflection to the exact recovery recommendation and source lesson.\n- Record one metacognitive feedback event and show a clear boundary that it does not increase mastery.\n- Preserve the existing return path to the interrupted lesson.\n\n## Capabilities\n\n### New Capabilities\n\n- `evidence-neutral-recap-closure`: Closes a verified recap with learner-owned, bounded, non-mastery reflection evidence.\n\n### Modified Capabilities\n\n- None.\n\n## Impact\n\n- Training cabin recovery presentation and styling.\n- Lesson handoff reflection projection and tests.\n- Existing learning-event schema only; no new event kind, API, dependency, or backend migration.\n\n
